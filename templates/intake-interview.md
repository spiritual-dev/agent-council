# Intake interview

Ask one question at a time. Capture answers into the first `org.json`. Skip anything already answered.

1. What do you want this agent org to help you become or protect over the next year?
2. What are three values that should constrain every agent decision?
3. Who should be your master agent today (name of the bot or product you already talk to)?
4. Which life domains are active right now? (work/company, money, health, relationships, learning, home/admin, creative)
5. For each active domain: what is the one outcome that would make the next 90 days a win?
6. What tools and channels do you already use (calendar, mail, chat, notes, code, money apps)?
7. What must never happen without your explicit yes (send, spend, post, delete, share)?
8. Quiet hours and rest days?
9. Are you mainly: founder/builder, employee with a job, student, caregiver, or a mix?
10. How small should we start (recommend: master + 2 or 3 teams)?

When done: draft `org.json`, render the interface, show the map, and ask for approval before treating it as live.
